## Why This Was Built

Everything I build ultimately runs on electricity, and I'd never learned the fundamentals. Ohm's law,
what a capacitor actually does, why a resistor goes there — this is the bottom of the stack, and it's
satisfying to reach it.

Practical skills matter as much as theory here: breadboarding a circuit, soldering a joint that holds, and
using a multimeter to find out why something doesn't work instead of guessing.

## Why This Matters (Industry Application)

Hardware skills complement software ones and are increasingly relevant with IoT and edge devices. Being
able to diagnose whether a problem is electrical or logical — and to repair rather than replace — is a
genuinely useful, uncommon combination.

## Topics Covered

| Area | What this project covers |
|------|--------------------------|
| Fundamentals | Voltage, current, resistance, and Ohm's law |
| Components | Resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, LEDs |
| Breadboarding | Prototyping a circuit without soldering |
| Soldering | Technique, safety, and joints that last |
| Multimeter | Measuring voltage, continuity, and resistance |
| Debugging | Finding a fault systematically rather than by guess |
